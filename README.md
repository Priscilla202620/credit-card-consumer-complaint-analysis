# Credit Card Consumer Complaint Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyses credit card consumer complaints to understand the main reasons customers complain and how companies respond to those complaints.

The analysis was completed using Microsoft Excel and is based on 21,293 credit card complaints.

## Business Question

What are the main reasons for credit card complaints, and where can financial service companies focus on improving customer experience?

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables
- Excel Charts

## Analysis Performed

The analysis focused on:

- Most common complaint issues
- Companies with the highest number of complaints in the dataset
- Company responses to complaints
- Timely responses
- Complaint submission methods

## Key Findings

- Purchase-related statement issues were the most common complaint, with 7,169 complaints (34%).
- Capital One had the highest number of complaints in the dataset, with 3,498 complaints (16%).
- 61% of complaints were closed with an explanation.
- 99% of complaints received a timely response.
- 92% of complaints were submitted through the web.

## Recommendations

- Make purchase information on customer statements clearer and easier to understand.
- Keep the online complaint process simple and easy for customers to use.
- Continue responding to complaints on time and focus on complaints that are delayed.

## Limitations

- The analysis covers only a three-month period.
- Complaint numbers alone cannot be used to determine which company performs worst because companies may have different numbers of customers.
- The analysis is limited to the information available in the complaint dataset.
